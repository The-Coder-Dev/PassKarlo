# 09 — AI System Direction

## Important distinction

### AI-assisted development

Antigravity or another coding agent may be used to build PassKarlo.

### AI-powered product

AI features inside PassKarlo are a future product capability.

These are separate concepts.

## Future AI capabilities

Potential future features:
- Natural-language education search
- Career recommendations
- Course recommendations
- Institute discovery
- Query understanding
- Personalized education guidance
- Summaries of structured institute/career information

## Grounding requirement

AI should be grounded in PassKarlo's structured data wherever possible.

AI must not invent:
- Fees
- Eligibility
- Institute facts
- Rankings
- Admissions
- Course availability

## V1

Do not add AI-powered product features unless explicitly requested.

Do not add an AI chat interface simply because the product may eventually use AI.
